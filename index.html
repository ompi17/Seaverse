Target: 2 courses a month

Everyone measured (sea staff, trainees and office staff) has the same target: 2 courses a month, about one every 15.2 days.

Basis: PF 05B Computer Based Training (Seaverse) Matrix, compulsory (C) courses only for the rank, COMMON plus the vessel-type matrix. Monthly need = 3-year courses ÷ 36 + 5-year courses ÷ 60; one-time courses and recommended (R) courses are left out. This works out at about 1.2 a month (catering) to 2.0 (junior deck officers); for example, a junior deck officer on a bulk carrier: 40 ÷ 36 + 56 ÷ 60 = 2.04. It is an average across the validity cycle, used here as a planning pace rather than course-by-course due dates.

Only sea staff whose Seaverse status is onboard on an active fleet vessel are analysed; people ashore or assigned are covered on the Off-board crew tab. Every onboard person is at one of these stages:

  • Not registered: invited, but hasn't completed registration yet.
  • Registered: accepted the invitation and signed in, but hasn't started a course yet. Not assessable.
  • Engaged: has started a first course on Seaverse, or has course history from the previous service provider. These are the assessable crew.
  • Active: engaged and has completed at least one course in the last 90 days (the rolling window).

The toggle picks who is counted, from widest to narrowest. The page opens on Registered. Drilling a table down to its list of people switches to All onboard, and going back up returns to the earlier view, unless you pick a different one yourself.

  • All onboard: every invitee. Crew not yet assessable count as 0%, so this shows the whole fleet's position, registration gaps included.
  • Registered: crew who have completed registration (with or without a course started). Registered crew who haven't started yet count as 0%.
  • Engaged: only the assessable crew, with nothing filled in as 0%.
  • Active: engaged crew with at least one course completed in the last 90 days.

So the figures rise from All onboard to Active. Headcounts, coverage and the funnel always cover everyone onboard.

Individual score (same model as v24)

  • First course start (E): the earliest course start. Records brought over from the previous provider without a start date count as starting on 1 Nov 2025 (the Seaverse roll-out).
  • Eligible days: from the later of the 90-day window start and E, up to the reporting date (31 Oct 2026), both days included. Expected = eligible days ÷ 15.22 (2 courses a month = 2 per 30.44 days).
  • A · Frequency: courses completed in those eligible days ÷ expected × 100%. Not capped. Completions an admin marked complete (because of an app glitch) count like any other.
  • B · Lifetime: completed ÷ assigned × 100%.
  • Final = the higher of A and B. Compliant at 100% or more.
  • Not assessable: invitees who haven't registered and registered crew who haven't started a course. They have no score of their own; in the All onboard and Registered views they count as 0%, and the Engaged and Active views leave them out.

Vessel, DoC and fleet compliance is the average of the final scores of the people counted. A few very active people can lift the average, so also check the share at target. Bands: on target 100%+, near 75–99%, below 50–74%, low 1–49%, none 0%.

Example: someone who first started a course 40 days ago and completed 4 since then, with 9 of 20 assigned courses done: expected = 40 ÷ 15.22 = 2.63, so A = 4 ÷ 2.63 = 152%, B = 45%, final = 152% (on target).

Who is in which group

By the vessel each person is assigned to in Seaverse. Office staff are on the "Office Staff" record. Trainees are on the training ship record ("Trainee Vessel"). Everyone else is sea staff. Test accounts and the Sea Learning team's own accounts are left out.

Trainees and office staff

Scored exactly like sea staff (same stages, same 2-courses-a-month target, same counting toggle), but everyone in the group is counted, not just people onboard. Each page has a drill-down table: all ranks, then the people in a rank, with the same columns as Fleet summary. They will also be measured against a set of mandatory courses per rank once that list is supplied: mandatory completion = mandatory courses completed ÷ required, with each person's mandatory courses shown as completed, in progress, not started or not assigned.

Engagement filter

The Engagement filter in each table and the All onboard / Registered / Engaged / Active toggle are linked: changing one changes the other. The filter also offers single stages (not registered only, registered with no course yet, engaged with nothing in 90 days); picking one sets the toggle to the view that contains it.

Off-board crew

Sea staff whose status is not onboard: assigned to join a ship, on leave (onshore), or with no status set. Same stages as onboard crew. Assigned crew are scored exactly like onboard crew (2 courses a month, final = higher of frequency and lifetime) so they arrive compliance-ready; on leave crew have no target and are monitored on registration, activity in the last 90 days, lifetime completion (completed ÷ assigned) and courses left open more than 90 days. Last vessel and DoC come from the vessel still tagged on the profile; people with none are shown as "Not tagged". These people are not included in any onboard figure.

Feedback

Feedback forms submitted by learners after a course. The overall star rating (1–5) is averaged per course and group; “satisfied” means 4–5 stars. Feedback rate = course completions that got a feedback form back ÷ completions since feedback was switched on for that course. Written answers are cleaned of filler (“nil”, “good”, “.”) and tagged into themes by keywords, so a comment can sit in more than one theme; “issues flagged” are comments about quiz grading or connectivity. Multiple-choice answers will be added once the export includes the selected options.

Dashboard tab

The Dashboard tab uses the same per-person scores as the Fleet summary. Gauges, bands and the DoC chart follow the counting toggle and the DoC selector. Lifetime progress (completed ÷ assigned, pooled) is shown separately against an 80% target. The lower half of the Dashboard tab shows courses completed per person per week (target 0.46, i.e. 2 a month) and per month (target 2.0), the 13-week compliance trend, and daily completions for the last 90 days, all for today's onboard crew. Login volume isn't shown, because the export has no login data.

Vessels

Every active vessel is listed, including those with no crew registered or onboard on Seaverse. DoC is the company the vessel is registered to.

Data

Seaverse database export of 22 Oct 2026 plus the daily incremental updates to 1 Nov 2026, 13:39 IST / 08:09 UTC (the later run of each day, which includes the earlier one); reporting date 31 Oct 2026 (the last full day), with the 13-week trend at weekly reporting dates before it. Completions migrated from the previous provider count by their completion date but don't set the engagement date.
