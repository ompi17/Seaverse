What this site adds

A companion to the Seaverse Dashboard, for DoC, fleet, LMS and content admins. It uses the same people and groups (sea staff onboard, off-board sea staff, trainees, office staff).

Learning plans

Active learning plans and the conditions that put people in them (rank, vessel type, or a group). For each plan: how many people in the current filters are enrolled, onboard and off-board, and how many of the plan's compulsory courses are done and in date. Opening a plan lists its courses; that course table follows the Category and DoC filters. People with no plan are in scope but enrolled in no active plan, so nothing is assigned to them.

Filters

Filters are shared by the Learning plans, Compulsory training, Certificates and Logins tabs. Each list only offers values that exist with the other filters; picking a vessel also sets its DoC; × clears that filter (clearing a DoC also clears its vessel). Clicking a DoC row sets the DoC filter and shows its vessels; clicking a vessel shows its people. Use All crew / Onboard / Off-board beside the table to split a DoC or vessel by where people are now (off-board people are listed under their last vessel).

Compulsory training

Learning plans assign courses by rank and vessel type, with each course tagged Assigned (compulsory, the matrix's C) or Recommended (R). A person's compulsory courses are the Assigned courses of every active plan they are enrolled in. Done = completed at least once, on Seaverse or with the previous provider. Up to date = done and not past its validity (courses with no expiry count once done).

Certificates and validity

Each course's validity comes from its certificate set-up (3 years, 5 years, or no expiry). The latest completion, on Seaverse or from the previous provider (matched by email), plus the validity gives the expiry date. Status at the reporting date: Valid, Due within 30, 60 or 90 days, Expired, or Not yet done. The Courses selector on that tab switches between compulsory courses (default), recommended courses, or both. Records migrated from the previous provider show as “Previous provider”.

Logins and app use

Last login from the user record; App = has a mobile push token. Dormant = registered but not logged in for over 30 days. Until the daily users file carries the last-login field, this tab shows the 16 Oct snapshot.

Content

Structure (lessons, videos, PDFs, quizzes, questions, pass mark, minutes) from the latest published version of each course. Video languages count all language versions of the course videos; subtitle languages come from the subtitle files. Nationality is read from the employee ID prefix (IN, PH, CN, ID…).
